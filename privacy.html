Pathpolio: Privacy Policy

What the app collects

Only what the student types or uploads: name, student ID, class, and optionally school, city or province, email, phone, links, an ID document link, a profile photo, and the CV or portfolio content (education, projects, achievements, images and links).

Where it is stored

Everything is stored only in the browser of the student’s own device. Pathpolio does not send it to an EBA server. The student can delete it at any time with the “Clear” buttons.

How it is shared

Nothing is shared unless the student chooses to share it. The PDF is created by the student’s own browser. In the “Share With Us” window the student can open Gmail or an email app with a prepared message to the EBA address, or copy the address. The app does not attach the PDF automatically; the student attaches it. Email is not end-to-end encrypted.

Children and young people

Students under 18 may share their portfolio only with the agreement of a parent or guardian. The app asks the student to confirm this before the sharing options are enabled.

Third-party code

Pathpolio does not load third-party scripts, fonts or analytics. Links that the student adds (for example Google Drive or YouTube) open the student’s own pages on those services.

Your choices and contact

You can delete your data on your device at any time. To ask about a portfolio that was sent to EBA, contact [EBA contact email].
